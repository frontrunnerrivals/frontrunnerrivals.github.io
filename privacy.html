Front Runner Rivals Privacy Policy

App: Front Runner Rivals (com.frontrunnerrivals.game) · Android and iOS · Effective date: 1 November 2026

Front Runner Rivals is an arcade horse racing game. This policy explains what information the game handles. In short: we do not sell your personal data and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Ads come from Google AdMob, purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, the global leaderboard runs on Microsoft PlayFab, and usage statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase.

Information we collect

The game has no sign-in and never asks for your real name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. It creates a random player ID on your device for the leaderboard. When you first open the game you choose a racing name (your own name or a nickname), and you may pick a country. The services below receive the data described in their sections.

Data stored on your device

Your career progress, unlocked tracks and horses, best race times, daily race results, missions, achievements, settings and your racing name are saved in the game's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad after some completed races, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example "Last Chance"). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data, under Google's partner policy. On iOS the game does not ask for permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or its ad partners. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent first through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later with the "Privacy choices" button in the store screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Buying "Remove Ads" or the full bundle turns off the automatic full-screen ads; optional rewarded videos remain available.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never see your payment details. The game receives only a purchase confirmation from the store. On Android, this confirmation (product, transaction token and the store's signature) is sent together with your random player ID to Microsoft PlayFab, which checks it with Google before anything is unlocked. On iOS, the App Store confirms the purchase directly to the game. Purchased items are recorded on your device and can be restored from the store after reinstalling.

Global rankings (Microsoft PlayFab)

The global leaderboard is hosted on Microsoft Azure PlayFab. When you finish a race with a new best time, the game sends a random player ID created on your device, your racing name, the country you picked (optional), the track, your best time and a random race token. PlayFab also receives your IP address as part of the connection. This is used only to rank players, reject impossible or duplicate times and show the leaderboard. Your name, country and total time are visible to other players. Racing names that are offensive or impersonate others may be removed. To have your ranking and PlayFab account deleted, email us with your racing name; uninstalling the game removes the local player ID.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game may use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand basic usage (for example app opens, completed races, Last Chance use and purchases) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (device model, OS version, crash details). In regions where consent is required, analytics and advertising storage stay off unless you agree in the consent form; you can change this with "Privacy choices". Crash reports are used only to fix bugs. Data is processed by Google under Firebase's privacy terms and kept for Google's standard retention periods (Analytics up to 14 months, Crashlytics 90 days).

Delete your data

Front Runner Rivals has no user accounts. To delete your data:

  • Email frontrunnerrivals@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your racing name (and country, if you picked one).
  • We delete your PlayFab player record, leaderboard entries and the purchase checks linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device (progress, settings, player ID).

Google Play and the Apple App Store keep their own order records for purchases as required by law. Firebase analytics and crash data are deleted automatically after Google's retention periods.

App stores

The game is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. Google and Apple may process information about your download and device under their own privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change and show the new effective date above.
